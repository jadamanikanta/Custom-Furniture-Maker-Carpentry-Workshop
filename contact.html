HAVE QUESTIONS?

Before You Get Started.

Here are a few things customers commonly ask before placing a custom furniture enquiry.

How does the custom order process work?

Submit your requirements through the enquiry form. We'll review your project, discuss the details with you, and then work through the design, materials, pricing and production timeline.

Can I request specific dimensions?

Yes. Custom dimensions are welcome. Include your approximate measurements in the enquiry form and we'll discuss the suitable proportions with you.

Can you help me choose the right material?

Absolutely. If you're unsure about the material or finish, mention it in your enquiry and our team can help you select an option based on the furniture, usage and desired appearance.

Can I share reference images?

Yes. Reference images, sketches and inspiration examples can help us understand your preferred style and overall direction.

How long does a custom order take?

The timeline depends on the furniture type, dimensions, materials, finish and quantity. We'll provide an estimated timeline after reviewing your requirements.
