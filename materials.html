CARE & MAINTENANCE

Keep The Character.

Good furniture becomes more beautiful with time. A few simple habits help preserve the timber, finish and craftsmanship.

CRAFTED TO AGE WITH YOU
01

Keep It Dry

Wipe spills promptly and avoid leaving standing water on wooden surfaces.

02

Use Soft Cloths

Clean surfaces gently with a soft, slightly damp cloth to protect the finish.

03

Avoid Direct Heat

Keep timber away from prolonged direct heat and strong sunlight where possible.

04

Refresh The Finish

Depending on the finish, occasional maintenance can restore its original warmth and protection.
